EXAMPLE DATA (advanced version)
===============================
A small subset of the validation photographs (Samsung Galaxy S25 FE, Pro mode: ISO 100, 1/125 s,
fixed white balance, manual focus; 20 Nov 2026), renamed for convenience. All photographs, with their
original time-stamp names, are in ../../photos/raw/ern_photos (see ../../photos/photo_sets.md).

photos/
  00_dark.jpg               lamp blocked, same settings         (20261120_002855)
  01_reference_ethanol.jpg  reference: ethanol in the cuvette    (20261120_003701)
  02_DCM_49uM.jpg           DCM 49.5 uM in ethanol               (20261120_003349)
  03_sample1.jpg ... 07_sample5.jpg   2026 mixed-dye set: violet, cyan, magenta, pink, lime
                            (20261120_003411, _003429, _003450, _003509, _003526)

uvvis/  laboratory UV-Vis spectra of the same solutions, 1 cm cuvettes (CSV: wavelength nm, absorbance, SD):
  sample1.csv ... sample5.csv, DCM_49uM.csv, ethanol.csv

Laboratory band maxima: DCM 470 nm; sample 1 587 nm; sample 3 560 nm; sample 4 545 nm;
samples 2 (about 650 nm) and 5 (about 410 nm) lie at the ends of the phone's usable range.

The example photographs of the basic version (event, automatic exposure) are in the basic record,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23400260.
